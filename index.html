Page Saver – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Page Saver is a Chrome extension that saves pages, recipes, videos and posts you choose, and can summarize them with AI. This policy explains what happens to your data.

The short version

  • Everything you save stays on your computer, in your browser's local storage. The developer has no server and never receives your saved items, your browsing, or any personal information.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling of data.
  • The only time content leaves your computer is if you choose to connect your own Google Gemini API key – then the text of an item you save is sent to Google to be summarized.

What the extension handles

DataWhenWhere it goes
Content of a page, post, video or image you save (title, text, link, images)Only when you click save, use the right-click menu or press the shortcutStored locally in your browser (IndexedDB). Not sent to the developer.
Your settings and the number of items savedAlwaysStored locally (chrome.storage).
Your Gemini API key (optional)Only if you enter oneStored locally; sent only to Google, with each summary request.

The extension does not read pages in the background. It only reads a page when you ask it to save something from it. On YouTube, Facebook and Instagram it also shows a save button or a confirmation message on the page.

AI summaries

  • Local AI (Gemini Nano): runs on your computer through Chrome's built-in AI. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Gemini API (optional): if you connect your own API key, the text of a saved item is sent to Google's Gemini API to create the summary. Google's Gemini API terms and privacy policy apply to that request. On Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products.
  • You can turn AI off completely in the settings.

Sharing

The developer does not collect, sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. The only third party that can receive content is Google, and only through the optional Gemini API key you provide yourself, as described above.

Your control

  • Delete any saved item from the library at any time.
  • Export your items (Markdown or JSON) from the library.
  • Removing the extension deletes all of its locally stored data.

Children

Page Saver is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date. If a future version adds a paid subscription, this policy will be updated to describe the license check before that version is released.
